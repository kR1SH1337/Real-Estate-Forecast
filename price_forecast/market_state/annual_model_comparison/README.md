## Что не образует отдельную историческую строку

- Original mechanical scenario: forecast starts October 2026, no fitted history
- Linked, coupled and future-kappa scenarios share market_mos_lag1 historical equation
- Sales-target inversion and price corridors have no independent historical price equation
- Sensitivity windows, omitted years and alternative initial values are checks, not additional main model families
